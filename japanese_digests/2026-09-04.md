## 1. 生涯学習のユーキャンがデジタル集音器参入 高音域を補う「テルマホン」

- **Outlet:** Impress Watch
- **Publication date:** 2026-09-03
- **Link:** https://www.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2138032.html
- **Topic:** 製品・暮らし（デジタル集音器）
- **Article type:** product news
- **Tone:** neutral and informative
- **Why it is worth teaching:** Shows the standard Japanese pattern for a product announcement: releasing company, date, product name, price, sales channel, and colors. The predictable structure makes it easy for N4/N3 learners to understand the main points and vocabulary.
- **Why ordinary viewers may care:** Digital hearing aids are a real-life product category in aging Japan, and students also need to be able to read simple commercial/organization announcements.
- **Language value:** Introduces polite written-style business phrases, number reading (39,800円), katakana product names, and modifiers such as 耳穴をふさがない.
- **Suggested video angle:** Read the short text aloud, highlight the product-news structure, then practice using the same patterns for other imaginary products.
- **Suggested expressions to teach:** デジタル集音器, 〜を発売する, 耳穴をふさがない, 価格は〜円, 通販サイトで販売する
- **Estimated difficulty:** N3
- **Estimated video length:** 5 min
- **Seems publicly accessible:** likely public
- **Priority score:** 8/10
